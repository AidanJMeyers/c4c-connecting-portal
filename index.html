What Connections 4 Change is

Connections 4 Change is run by Counselors for Change, Inc. (C4C). It introduces licensed mental health professionals ("presenters") to schools and organizations that want an educational session. C4C verifies each presenter's license and insurance details and then lets the two sides connect directly.

C4C connects; it does not employ or insure presenters

Presenters are independent professionals. They are not employees or agents of C4C, and they present under their own professional liability insurance. C4C's insurance does not cover them. Sessions are educational and are not therapy or clinical services.

No independent background checks

C4C checks license and insurance information but does not run background checks. Organizations remain responsible for their own vetting, especially for events involving minors.

What we collect

From presenters: name, email, phone (optional), city and state, license type, state and number, years practicing, expertise, audiences, session types, delivery, availability windows, timezone, minimum notice, a short bio, an optional website, and liability insurance details (carrier, policy number, limits, expiration).

From organizations: organization name and type, lead contact name and email, optional CC emails, and for each request the session type, length, delivery, location, expected attendance, audience, age range, topics, date window, description and optional support level.

How we use it

  • To list verified presenters in a searchable directory.
  • To route requests, introduce both sides, and send updates by email.
  • To verify license and insurance information before and after a profile is approved.
  • To report aggregate, non-identifying statistics about C4C's impact.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Account and profile data is stored in a hosted database (Supabase) with access rules that enforce the table above. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Emails are sent from C4C's own mail account.

Eligibility

You must be 18 or older to create an account. Do not include information about individual students or other minors in a request.

Your choices

You can edit your profile or organization details at any time from your account. To see, correct or delete everything we hold about you, email info@counselorsforchange.org.

Support levels and donations

Selecting a support level expresses an intended contribution and is not a payment. Before soliciting donations online, C4C will confirm its charitable solicitation registration in the relevant states.
