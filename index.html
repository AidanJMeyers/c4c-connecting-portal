Before you sign up

  • Your name, credentials, expertise and bio are public once approved.
  • Your license number and insurance are used for verification only. We check your license automatically and recheck it monthly.
  • After you submit, you'll get an email to set up your login so you can edit your profile at any time.
  • You share general availability windows. Once you connect with an organization, you settle the exact date together.
  • What you get: visibility in your community, a ready-made slide template and resource bank, and a way to give back.

What we collect

From licensed professionals: name, email, phone (optional), city/state, license type, license state, license number, years practicing, expertise, audiences, session formats, delivery, availability windows, timezone, minimum notice, a short bio, an optional website, and professional liability insurance details (carrier, policy number, limits and expiration).

From organizations: organization name and type, contact name and email, event type, length, delivery, timezone, location or meeting link, expected attendees, audience, topics, proposed times, event description and selected support level.

How we use it

  • To list verified professionals in a searchable directory.
  • To match and route speaker requests, and follow up by email.
  • To verify license information before a profile is approved.
  • To report aggregate, non-identifying statistics.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

C4C connects; it does not employ or insure presenters

Presenters are independent professionals. They are not employees or agents of Counselors for Change, and they present under their own professional liability insurance. C4C's insurance does not cover them.

No independent background checks

C4C reviews self-reported license information but does not run background checks. Organizations remain responsible for their own vetting, especially for events involving minors.

Eligibility

You must be 18 or older to submit either form. Do not include information about individual students or other minors.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@counselorsforchange.org.

Donations

Selecting a support level expresses an intended contribution. Before soliciting donations online, confirm C4C's charitable solicitation registration in the relevant states.
